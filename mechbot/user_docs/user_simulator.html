Protocol Exploitation Cheat Sheet (mechfight/1)

  • 50ms Wall-Time Margin: Keep median decision latency under 8ms (1 CPU, 512MB RAM, no swap). Never run heavy neural/LLM loops per tick.
  • Trees Don't Block Shots: Fire blind into foliage along recent sound bearings or 5-second memory tracks!
  • 1v15 Practice vs 16-Way FFA: House bots share vision & hunt weakest-first without fighting each other. Don't overfit exclusively to passive camping—Practice rewards kiting & thinning weak Easy bots, while Finals reward third-partying and late-zone survival.
  • Cube Size Tradeoff: Stats cap at 10 cubes (+40% HP & Dmg), but hitbox radius grows +5% per cube uncapped. Avoid risky cube grabs past 10 cubes!
