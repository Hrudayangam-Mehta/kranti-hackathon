         Mech Royale Competitive Strategy Report
Research synthesis: game theory, battle royale design, AI agents, psychology, and strategic
integration.


Executive Summary
Winning strategy should optimize placement, survival probability, information advantage, and
endgame positioning rather than raw kill count.

Core Meta
Placement-only scoring shifts optimal behavior toward risk-aware decision making and consistency.

Information Warfare
Track sounds, enemy sightings, projectile trajectories, and inferred positions. Build probabilistic
enemy models.

Roof and Elevation Control
Prioritize rooftops for vision, loot access, safer rotations, and long-range engagements.

Weapon Framework
Laser for precision and endgame control, Mortar for area denial and disruption, Lightning for dense
close combat.

Supply Drop Strategy
Ambush over rush. Use drops as information traps and engagement funnels.

Behavior Architecture
Perception -> World Model -> Threat Map -> Utility Maximizer -> Action Selection.

Advanced Psychology
Exploit opponent greed, tunnel vision, over-commitment, and combat fixation. Let others create risk.

Final Recommendation
Focus on survival-first utility optimization, threat-aware movement, sound intelligence, and
low-variance placements.
Integrated Tactical Blueprint
Early Game: secure elevation, collect resources, avoid marginal fights.
Mid Game: gather intelligence, rotate strategically, exploit third-party engagements.
Late Game: maximize zone position and cover; selectively eliminate threats.
Final Four: shift from survival weighting to combat weighting while preserving positional advantage.

Suggested AI modules: Sound Tracker, Enemy Belief Engine, Threat Heatmaps, Loot Planner,
Zone Planner, Utility Arbiter, Combat Controller.
